# The twelve fields

v0.1.0 lists only each field's name and focus, with no specific knowledge. The content is generated by the AI the user connects.

| Field | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| Psychology | feelings, motives, cognitive biases |
| Relationships | trust, boundaries, communication |
| Sociology | groups, norms, social structures |
| Economics | incentives, costs, allocation of resources |
| Law | rights, duties, rules and risks |
| Politics & public policy | power, institutions, public interest |
| History | precedents and long-term trends |
| Ethics | different standards of right and wrong |
| Health & medicine | physical and mental health |
| Education & growth | learning and personal development |
| Media & communication | how information is presented and spread |
| Evolution & biology | instincts and evolutionary background |

## Planned "lens" notes

In the future, each field will have its own note, covering:
